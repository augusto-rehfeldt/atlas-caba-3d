# Third-party material

The CC0 1.0 dedication in `LICENSE` covers only the original code and text written
for this project. Building footprints, heights, streets, parks, barrio boundaries
and the 2021 aerial photography come from the Gobierno de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires
(Buenos Aires Data / IDECABA) and remain under their published terms. Renders and
datasets derived from them carry the same attribution requirements.
